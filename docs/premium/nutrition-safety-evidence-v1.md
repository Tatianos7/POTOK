Before numeric safety policy: obtain and review full authoritative evidence for
the intended population, anthropometric plausibility, activity/energy estimation,
deficit/surplus, protein/fat and carbohydrate availability, and contraindications.
Record source date, population, units/body-mass denominator, strength/limitations
and each concrete product rule. Reconcile inherited Goal bounds/formulas explicitly.
Independent mathematical checks (finite inputs, nonnegative residual, 4/9/4
accounting already used by POTOK) are not clinical safety approval.

Cycle contract currently follows the owner's requested opt-in, symptom-oriented
design. No phase prescription or medical diagnosis is implemented. Any health
warning copy and escalation rules need evidence/review before user-facing rollout.
